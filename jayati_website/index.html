Hi, I'm Jayati 👋

By day I'm a Data Scientist at Docplexus, a platform for doctors. Right now I'm mostly writing automation scripts that pull data from the web and turn it into clean, ready-to-use datasets for my team, so nobody has to copy-paste from endless browser tabs again. Before that, I built a self-serve reporting tool that saves my team hours every week, and a pipeline that matches doctor records against a huge database, which was about as fun as it sounds, and also kind of satisfying.

By night, I'm usually deep in AI, reading up on how LLMs and retrieval actually work, trying things out until they make sense, and then writing about them on Medium in plain language.

Fun fact: before data, I was the graphic design head of my college's computer science society, so yes, I care how charts look.

Currently: fresh off my MBA in Data Science, learning vector search and Databricks, and looking for my next role in Data Science or GenAI.
